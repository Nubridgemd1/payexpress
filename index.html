For IMTOs, banks & fintechs in Nigeria

Plug PayExpress into your payout rails

PayExpress originates and screens compliant USD transfers in the United States and hands you clean, funded, ready-to-pay instructions. Connect your IMTO or bank in Nigeria with a single REST API and start receiving payouts.

One REST API

Create payouts, check status, and reconcile with a documented JSON API and sandbox keys.

Webhooks

Real-time delivery, failure, and settlement events pushed to your endpoint.

Compliance at source

US-side KYC/AML/OFAC done before funds reach you - traceable, pre-screened flows.

FX & settlement

Agreed rate and margin per transfer; USD settlement to your nostro on a set cycle.

Any payout method

Bank (NUBAN), card, mobile wallet, or cash - route to whatever you support.

CBN-aligned

Built to operate within the CBN IMTO / authorised-dealer framework.
