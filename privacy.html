Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BizFormCorp LLC ("PayExpress", "we") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use the PayExpress money-transfer service. We handle your information in accordance with applicable US privacy and financial-data laws, including the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) Safeguards Rule.

1. Information we collect

  • Identity & contact: name, date of birth, address, email, phone, and government-ID details needed to verify you (KYC).
  • Transaction & payment: payment-card details (processed securely by our PCI-compliant processor), transfer amounts, recipients, and payout details.
  • Technical: device, log, and usage data to operate the service and prevent fraud.

2. How we use it

  • To process and deliver your transfers and provide customer support.
  • To verify your identity and meet Bank Secrecy Act / AML, OFAC, and other legal obligations.
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud and prohibited activity.
  • To improve and secure the service.

3. How we share it

We share information only as needed to provide the service and comply with law, including with:

  • Heritage Hub Federal Credit Union (NCUA #24972), the Transmitter of Record, for money transmission and BSA/AML compliance;
  • our payment processor(s) for card authorization and settlement;
  • the licensed IMTO and payout partners that deliver funds to your recipient;
  • regulators, law enforcement, and auditors where required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Card data security

Payment-card data is transmitted over encrypted connections and handled by a PCI-DSS-compliant processor. We do not store full card numbers on our own systems.

5. Data retention

We retain transaction and identity records for at least five years, or longer where required by the Bank Secrecy Act and other applicable record-keeping rules.

6. Your choices & rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal record-keeping obligations. Contact us using the details below. We will respond as required by applicable law.

7. Children

PayExpress is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted here with a revised date.
