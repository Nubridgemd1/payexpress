Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026
In short: Cancel within 30 minutes for a full refund (before the money is collected or deposited). If a transfer can't be completed, you're refunded in full to your original card. Completed transfers are final, and you're protected by Regulation E error-resolution rights.

1. Cancelling a transfer

You may cancel a transfer and receive a full refund within 30 minutes of payment, as long as the funds have not yet been picked up or deposited to the recipient. To cancel, open the transfer in the app or contact us using the details below.

2. Transfers that can't be completed

If a transfer cannot be delivered — for example, incorrect recipient details or a payout-partner issue — we will refund the full amount to your original payment card. Card refunds typically post within 5-10 business days, depending on your card issuer.

3. Completed transfers

Once funds have been delivered to the recipient, a transfer is final and cannot be reversed. Please double-check the recipient and amount before you pay — the exchange rate, charges, amount received, and delivery time are all shown before you confirm.

4. Your Regulation E rights (error resolution)

For remittance transfers, US federal law (the Remittance Rule under Regulation E) gives you the right to report an error within 180 days. If you think there was an error, contact us and we will investigate and, where appropriate, refund or re-send the transfer. You will receive a receipt and disclosures with your cancellation and error-resolution rights at the time of each transfer.

5. How to request a cancellation or refund

Email admin@bizformcorp.com or call (281) 935-9597 with your transfer reference number. We aim to acknowledge requests within one business day.

6. No fee to cancel

There is no charge to cancel an eligible transfer within the cancellation window.
