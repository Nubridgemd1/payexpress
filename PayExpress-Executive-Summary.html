Money Transfer to Nigeria - Executive Summary
ConfidentialProduct overview - Sept 2026

PayExpress is a licensed US-to-Nigeria money transfer platform that lets the diaspora send money home in minutes at a fair, transparent rate - paid to a bank account, card, mobile wallet, or cash pickup. It pairs a consumer app and card services with a real-time payout API that plugs straight into any IMTO or bank in Nigeria.

Why it wins

  • Nigeria receives an estimated ~US$20bn/yr in remittances (World Bank) - a large, recurring corridor.
  • Two-sided value: acquire the sender in the US and the recipient in Nigeria.
  • FX margin + fees on every transfer, plus card and deposit revenue.
  • Compliance done at source (US MSB, HHFCU-sponsored) - clean, pre-screened flows.

Card services

Give recipients a local Naira card for spending at home and diaspora customers an international USD card for online and travel - instant virtual issuance, controls and freeze, interchange revenue.

The IMTO settlement model

Real-time instructions, settle-then-credit. PayExpress pushes each payment instruction to the IMTO in real time, carrying a settlement confirmation. The IMTO credits the beneficiary only once the corresponding USD has settled in the US receivable account - eliminating pre-funding and payout risk. USD is remitted to the partner's nostro on the agreed cycle with a full reconciliation file.

Compliance

US origination is a FinCEN-registered Money Services Business with multi-state coverage, sponsored by Heritage Hub Federal Credit Union (HHFCU) (NCUA-insured); full BSA/AML/OFAC screening before funds move. Nigeria payout operates within the CBN IMTO / authorised-dealer framework.
